LeadPilot Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

LeadPilot is a WhatsApp assistant that helps businesses respond to customer inquiries.

What we collect

When you message a business using LeadPilot, we store your phone number, your messages, and details you share (such as budget, location and property preferences).

How we use it

Only to reply to you, follow up on your inquiry, and help the business serve you. We do not sell your data.
